Figure-data verification

Result: all 18 manuscript PNG assets for Figures 5-12 were reproduced with
zero differing pixels using the final seven-column CSV data and the original
figure styling and simulator screenshots.

CSV schema (English only):
figure: manuscript figure number.
panel: manuscript panel letter.
series: plotted curve, point set, boundary, or filled-surface component.
point: point order within that series, starting at 1.
x, y: final horizontal and vertical plotting coordinates.
z: third coordinate for 3D data; blank for 2D data.
Units follow the axes of the corresponding manuscript panel.

For example, Fig6 / c / ego_speed contains time in x and ego speed in y.
Select a figure, panel, and series and plot its coordinates in point order.
Do not smooth, scale, interpolate, filter, extend, or transform these values.
Filled regions and 3D mesh faces are already represented by their final
ordered vertices. Identical track geometry used by several cases is included
in each corresponding case file.

Only geometry actually drawn in the paper is included. Unplotted telemetry,
unused local plans, and intermediate tables have been removed. Imola and
Suzuka overview figures use track geometry and event locations; their
simulator views remain image assets rather than numerical CSV series.

Files: 4 Yas Marina cases, 4 Imola cases, 4 Suzuka cases, and Fig10.csv.
The data directory contains only these 13 CSV files, with no plotting code.

Pixel identity additionally requires the original fonts, colors, layout,
annotation formatting, simulator screenshots, and 600 dpi PNG export settings.
CSV alone cannot reconstruct simulator screenshot pixels or prescribe styling.
See verification.json for per-image pixel counts and final CSV checksums.
